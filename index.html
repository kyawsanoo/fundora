What is Fundora?

Fundora is a powerful crypto app that lets you track real-time cryptocurrency prices, earn and spend Credits points, and trade in both spot and futures markets using Credits.

Key Features

  • Real-Time Crypto Prices: Stay updated with live prices of your favorite cryptocurrencies.
  • Credits Points: Earn Credits in the app for activities, which can be used for trading or other rewards.
  • Spot & Futures Trading: Use CREDITS to trade in both spot and futures markets securely.
  • In-App Games: Play games to earn additional CREDITS and rewards.
  • Secure login and account protection.
