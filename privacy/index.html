Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 13, 2026

Fundora (“we”, “our”, “us”) values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use the Fundora mobile application (“App”).

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, account information, and transaction-related data if you choose to create an account or use in-app features.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you use the App, such as features accessed, session duration, interactions, and error reports.
  • Device & Technical Information: Device type, operating system, advertising ID, device identifiers, IP address, and approximate location derived from IP address.
  • Advertising Data: The App displays advertisements provided by third-party advertising partners. These partners may collect advertising identifiers, IP address, and app interaction data to deliver and measure ads.
  • Analytics: We use analytics tools to understand user behavior, improve performance, and enhance the user experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, operate, and improve Fundora’s services.
  • Process transactions and manage user accounts.
  • Respond to support requests and communicate important updates.
  • Analyze app performance and user behavior.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and ensure platform security.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. Sharing Your Information

  • Advertising Partners: We share limited data such as advertising ID and IP address with third-party advertising partners to display and measure ads.
  • Service Providers: Trusted third parties that assist with analytics, infrastructure, customer support, and app functionality.
  • Legal Compliance: When required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users and the company.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, user information may be transferred as part of the transaction.

4. Third-Party Services

Fundora uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. These services operate under their own privacy policies.

  • Appodeal (Advertising Network)
    Privacy Policy: https://www.appodeal.com/privacy-policy

5. Security of Your Information

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices

  • Access, update, or delete your account through the App.
  • Opt out of personalized ads through your device settings.
  • Contact us to request data deletion.

7. Request Data Deletion

Fundora respects your privacy and provides users with the ability to request deletion of some or all personal data without deleting your account.

How to request data deletion:

  • Email us at: kyawsanoo.androider@gmail.com
  • Include your registered email address and user ID (if available).
  • Specify which data you would like deleted.

Types of data that may be deleted:

  • Account profile information
  • Usage and activity data associated with your account

Types of data that may be retained:

  • Transaction records required for legal or financial compliance
  • Aggregated or anonymized analytics data

We will process verified deletion requests within a reasonable timeframe, subject to legal and regulatory requirements.

8. Children’s Privacy

Fundora is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
