Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and shared when you use our mobile applications: Fundora and Note Bazaar.

Fundora provides financial features, digital services, and reward-based activities.

Note Bazaar is a creative platform where users can create notes, share ideas, and participate in a marketplace using in-app points.

By using our applications (“Apps”), you agree to this Privacy Policy.

Apps Covered by This Policy

  • Fundora – financial services, rewards, and app activities.
  • Note Bazaar – note creation, idea sharing, and marketplace features.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and profile information if you create an account.
  • User Content (Note Bazaar): Notes, ideas, comments, and marketplace listings created by users.
  • Transaction Data: Records of reward credits, marketplace activity, and in-app transactions.
  • Usage Data: Features used, session duration, interactions, and app activity.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, device identifiers, Advertising ID, and IP address.
  • Diagnostics: Crash reports, performance logs, and error reports.
  • Approximate Location: Derived from IP address only.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide and operate our Apps.
  • Manage user accounts and transactions.
  • Create and manage notes and marketplace listings in Note Bazaar.
  • Provide reward systems and manage in-app credits.
  • Improve app performance and user experience.
  • Analyze app usage and engagement.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and security issues.
  • Comply with legal obligations.
