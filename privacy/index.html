Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 13, 2026

Fundora (“we”, “our”, “us”) values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Fundora mobile app (“App”).

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, account info, and payment/transaction information.
  • Usage Data: App usage information such as features used, session duration, and errors.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers, and IP address.
  • Analytics & Cookies: We may use third-party analytics to understand app usage. No personal identifiers are collected.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and improve Fundora’s services.
  • Process transactions.
  • Communicate important updates or customer support responses.
  • Personalize your app experience.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. Sharing Your Information

  • Service Providers: Trusted third parties assisting with services (analytics, payment processing).
  • Legal Compliance: When required by law or to protect legal rights.
  • Business Transfers: In case of merger, acquisition, or sale of Fundora.

4. Security of Your Information

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data. While we strive to secure your information, no method is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Choices

  • Update or delete your account through the App.
  • Opt-out of marketing communications via email or in-app settings.

6. Children’s Privacy

Fundora is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top indicates the last changes. Continued use of the App after updates indicates your acceptance.
