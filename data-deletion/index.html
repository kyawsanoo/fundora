Fundora – Data Deletion Request

Fundora respects your privacy and provides users with the ability to request deletion of certain personal data without deleting their account.

How to request data deletion

To request deletion of your data, please contact us using one of the following methods:

  • Email us at: support@fundora.com

Information to include in your request

  • Your registered email address
  • Your Fundora user ID (if available)
  • The type of data you would like deleted

Data that may be deleted

  • Account profile information
  • Usage and activity data associated with your account

Data that may be retained

  • Transaction records required for legal or financial compliance
  • Aggregated or anonymized analytics data

Processing time

We will process verified deletion requests within a reasonable timeframe, subject to legal and regulatory requirements.

If you have any questions, please contact us at support@fundora.com.
